About

What I value as a designer is turning ideas in people's minds or words into screens and real interactions.

Design issues are not always easy to explain only with words, and each person may imagine them differently.
That is why I organize stakeholders' ideas and make them easier to discuss through screen proposals and prototypes.

Based on proposals and prototypes, I communicate with people from different roles, such as business, development, and operations, and improve ideas step by step.

Through this process, I want to help projects and products move forward little by little.
